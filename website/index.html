03 Space Analyzer

Find out what's taking up space.

Rile measures a folder once, then lets you drill into it as a bubble map, list its largest files and review what can safely go.

  • Developer build output — node_modules, target, DerivedData
  • Caches & logs — apps rebuild them when needed
  • Installers & disk images — usually done once installed
  • Old downloads & stale files — surfaced, never auto-deleted

Nothing is removed until you review it, and everything goes to the Trash.

05 On-device intelligence

Intelligence that never
leaves orbit.

Search by meaning, summarize folders and describe images with models that run on your own machine — Apple Foundation Models on supported Macs, or local llama.cpp and MLX models.

Ask Rile ⌘K

Where did I save the Europa ice charts?

Illustrative conversation
  1. search_files"europa ice"3 results
  2. list_folderMission Control/Planets5 items
  3. read_fileMission Brief.mddone

The chart is Europa.jpg in Mission Control › Planets. The Mission Brief lists it under “Chart surface ice on Europa at 2 m resolution”.
